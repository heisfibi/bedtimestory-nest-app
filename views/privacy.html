Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13-10-2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fibi Bedtime Stories (the "Bedtime Stories" app), made by Fibi Technologies. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what the app sends to our server, what stays on your device, and what our story studio keeps about the people who write the books.

Fibi Bedtime Stories is designed for children. It shows no ads and contains no advertising or third-party analytics tools.

2. What the App Sends to Our Server

  • A random install number. The app makes it up the first time it runs. It is not your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID or any number belonging to the device; it only tells our server that two reports came from the same copy of the app.
  • Reading activity. Which books are opened and when, how many of their pages are read, the time spent on them, whether the end was reached, and whether the book was read, listened to or recorded.
  • Subscription status. Whether this copy of the app has an active subscription, which plan it is, and the purchase token Google Play gives for it (a reference to the purchase, not payment details).

We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, the advertising ID, or anything else that identifies a child or a parent.

3. How We Use It

  • To see which books are read, and how much, so we can improve the library.
  • To share subscription money with the creators of the books subscribers read: each creator's share depends on how much their books were read.
  • To check subscriptions with Google Play.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. It is stored on servers rented from our hosting provider, which handles it only on our behalf.

4. What Stays on Your Device

  • Voice recordings made with the app, and the reader's name typed for them, are stored only on the device and are never sent to us.
  • Downloaded books, music and settings are also kept only on the device.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are bought and paid for through Google Play (or Apple's App Store on Apple devices). We never see card or bank details. In the app, buying is behind a grown-up check. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Children’s Privacy

Fibi Bedtime Stories is designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The install number and reading activity described above are not linked to a name, an email address or a device identifier, and are used only to run the app and pay the creators of its books. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

7. Creators Using Our Studio

If you apply to become a creator, we keep what you write in the application (your name, email address, what you would like to make, the languages and country you give, what you tell us about yourself and any links), a scrambled (hashed) form of the password you choose, and whether it was approved or declined. Our team uses it only to decide on your application.

If you write books in the Fibi Bedtime Stories studio on our website, we keep your name, your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password to run your account, together with the books you upload and how much they earn. We use this only to run the studio and to work out what you are owed.

8. Security and Keeping Information

Information travels between the app and our server over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Reading reports are kept for as long as we need them to work out and record payments to creators. Because the install number is not linked to you, we cannot look up a person's reading by name; uninstalling the app stops any further reports.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
