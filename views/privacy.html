Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 06-01-2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fibi Bedtime Stories! Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is handled within the app.

Fibi Bedtime Stories is designed for children and complies with applicable privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), where applicable.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data or any information that directly identifies users. However, the following data may be collected or stored locally on the user’s device:

  • Audio Recordings:
    • Purpose: Used for enhancing the app’s functionality (e.g., recording story narrations).
    • Storage: Audio recordings are stored locally on the user’s device and are not transmitted to our servers.
    • User Consent: Recording is initiated by the user voluntarily.
  • Payment Information:
    • Purpose: For in-app purchases.
    • Handling: Payments are exclusively processed through Google Play and Apple’s App Store payment systems. We do not collect, store, or have access to users’ card details or payment information.

3. How We Use Information

All data collected by the app is used solely for its intended purpose:

  • Audio recordings are used to personalize the app experience and remain on the user’s device.
  • Payment information is handled by third-party payment processors for in-app purchases.

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party services, such as Google Play and Apple’s App Store, for payment processing. These services have their own privacy policies, which govern the handling of payment data. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

We do not collect or transmit any data to our servers. Data stored locally on the device is the responsibility of the user. We encourage users to secure their devices with appropriate measures, such as passwords or biometric locks.

6. Children’s Privacy

Fibi Bedtime Stories is designed for children and does not collect any personal information from users. The app fully complies with privacy regulations, including COPPA, ensuring a safe and secure experience.

7. User Rights

Since we do not store data on our servers, there is no data for users to request access to, modify, or delete. Users have full control over any data stored locally on their devices.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any updates will be communicated through the app or on our website.
